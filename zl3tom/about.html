About the operator

Kia ora, I’m Thomas.

Amateur radio operator, broadcaster, Toastmaster, and technology enthusiast based in Christchurch, New Zealand.

My callsigns are ZL3TOM and ZL3KY. I've been licensed since 2018, and I enjoy talking on the radio and making contacts around the world.

Beyond the radio

  • I operate on many amateur radio modes.
  • I host a live FM radio show at Plains FM in Christchurch.
  • I'm an active Toastmaster.
  • I enjoy technology and helping other people use it.
  • I volunteer with Hāpai Access Card and TechMate.
