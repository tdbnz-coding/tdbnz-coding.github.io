QSL & log confirmations

I’m happy to confirm contacts through QRZ Logbook or eQSL. Here’s what I need to match your QSO.

Three simple steps

  1. 1
    Log the QSO

    Add the contact to your QRZ Logbook or eQSL.

  2. 2
    Send the details

    Email the UTC date and time, band or network, mode, and your callsign.

  3. 3
    I’ll confirm it

    I’ll match the contact in my log, add a comment, and confirm.

Good to know — when I log a contact, I generally send the QSO details to the operator's QRZ email address to make confirmation easy.
