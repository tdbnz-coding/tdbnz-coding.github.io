Antenna Basics

Simple antenna fundamentals that help you get better results without overcomplicating your station.

All guides
01

The antenna matters most

A better antenna system usually improves results more than upgrading radios. Start simple, get on air, then improve one thing at a time.

02

Three basics that always help

  • Height helps, especially for VHF and UHF.
  • Clear space helps — stay away from metal roofs, gutters, and power lines.
  • Good feedline helps, especially on VHF and above where coax loss can be high.
03

Coax and connectors

  • Choose coax that suits the run length and band.
  • Weatherproof every outdoor join.
  • Use strain relief so connectors do not carry weight.
04

Understanding SWR

  • Low SWR can protect your radio and keep power transfer efficient.
  • Perfect SWR does not guarantee performance; pattern and height matter too.
  • If SWR is high, stop and check connectors, coax, and antenna length.
ON-AIR EXAMPLE
Fix safety and reliability first, tune for SWR second, then chase performance.
05

Safety comes first

  • Keep antennas well clear of power lines — always.
  • Secure masts and guy lines for serious wind loads.
  • Plan grounding and lightning protection for your situation.
Good operating

Listen first. Identify clearly. Leave a pause. Be patient and welcoming.
