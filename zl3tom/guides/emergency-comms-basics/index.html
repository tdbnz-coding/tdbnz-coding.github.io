ZL3TOM guide

Emergency Comms Basics

Clear, calm habits for message passing, priority traffic, and directed nets.

All guides
01

The goal: clear communication

In emergency-style traffic, clarity and calm matter more than radio jargon. Keep messages short, accurate, and confirmed.

02

Directed nets

  • The net control station manages the flow; wait to be called in.
  • Use short transmissions, identify clearly, then stand by.
  • If you double with someone, stop and try again when invited.
ON-AIR EXAMPLE
Net control, ZL3TOM, Christchurch, no traffic.
03

Message handling

  • Record who it is to, who it is from, and the message content.
  • Repeat names, addresses, phone numbers, and counts.
  • Read back key parts and confirm what you copied.
ON-AIR EXAMPLE
To: [name or agency]
From: [name or station]
Message: [short, clear content]
Time: [UTC]  Date: [UTC]
04

Priority and emergency breaks

Use priority or emergency breaks only when genuinely needed. Keep the wording short so net control can respond quickly.

ON-AIR EXAMPLE
Break, priority traffic
Break, emergency traffic
05

Voice discipline

  • Use phonetics for names and locations when copy is weak.
  • Pause between overs, especially on linked systems.
  • If you are relaying, clearly say so and identify who you are relaying for.
  • Never guess callsigns, names, or numbers.
Good operating

Listen first. Identify clearly. Leave a pause. Be patient and welcoming.
