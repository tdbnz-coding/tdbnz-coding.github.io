ZL3TOM guide

Repeaters and Nets

Use amateur radio repeaters and participate in nets confidently, including AllStar and EchoLink linked systems.

All guides
01

Repeater basics

  • Listen first to make sure the repeater is clear.
  • Key the microphone, pause for a second, then speak.
  • Identify clearly and leave pauses between overs.
  • Keep transmissions within the repeater timeout.
02

Monitoring and calling

If nobody replies, wait and try once more. If the repeater is busy, keep transmissions short.

ON-AIR EXAMPLE
ZL3ABC, ZL3ABC, this is ZL3TOM.
03

Joining a conversation

  • Wait for a clear gap, then say your callsign once.
  • If invited in, keep your first over short.
  • If it is a directed net or important traffic, stand by.
04

Linked systems

  • Allow extra pauses for network delay.
  • Keep topics appropriate for a wide audience.
  • Avoid kerchunking; identify briefly if you key accidentally.
  • Remember that many operators share the link.
05

Checking into a net

Wait for net control to request check-ins. Give your callsign, location, and whether you have traffic.

ON-AIR EXAMPLE
Net control, ZL3TOM, Christchurch, no traffic.
06

Passing messages

  • Write everything down before transmitting.
  • Read back important details to confirm accuracy.
  • Use phonetics for names, places, and numbers if copy is weak.
Good operating

Listen first. Identify clearly. Leave a pause. Be patient and welcoming.
