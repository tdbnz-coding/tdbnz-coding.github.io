GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We've got you.

A few things people often wonder before visiting for the first time.

Do I need public speaking experience?

Not at all. Toastmasters is a place to learn and practise. You can start wherever you are and build confidence step by step.

Do I have to speak on my first visit?

No. You're welcome to simply watch, listen and meet the members. If you'd like to participate, there may be an opportunity, but there's no pressure.

What happens at a typical meeting?

Meetings usually include prepared speeches, impromptu speaking, and helpful evaluations. Roles and activities help members practise communication and leadership.

How do I find the next meeting?

Use the official Toastmasters club finder to locate Avon Toastmasters and check the latest meeting time, venue and contact details before visiting.
