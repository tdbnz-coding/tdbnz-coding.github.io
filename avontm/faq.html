GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious about Toastmasters? Start here.

If you are thinking about visiting or joining Avon Toastmasters, these answers will help you know what to expect. Still wondering about something? Get in touch — we are happy to help.

VISITING & MEMBERSHIP

Frequently asked
questions.

There is no such thing as a silly question when you are trying something new.

Do I need to be good at public speaking to join?

Not at all. Many people join because they feel nervous or inexperienced. Toastmasters is a place to practise, learn and build confidence gradually. Everyone starts somewhere.

Can I visit before I become a member?

Absolutely. Guests are welcome to visit and experience a meeting before deciding. You can visit up to three times as a guest to help you decide whether the club is right for you. There is no hard sell and no obligation.

Is there a cost to visit?

No. Visiting as a guest is free. You can observe the meeting, meet members and ask questions without committing to membership.

What happens when I arrive?

Aim to arrive around 10–15 minutes before the 7:00 pm start if possible. A member can welcome you, explain the programme and help you find a seat. The meeting follows an agenda, so you will know what is happening as it goes along.

Will I have to give a speech or speak in front of everyone?

No. There is no requirement to speak during your first visit. Guests may simply watch and listen. If you feel comfortable, there may be an opportunity to briefly introduce yourself or try a Table Topics question, but participation is entirely your choice.

What should I bring or prepare?

Just bring yourself. You do not need to prepare a speech, bring equipment or have any previous Toastmasters experience. A notebook is optional.

Who typically joins Toastmasters?

Adults from many backgrounds and stages of life: professionals, students, business owners, people changing careers, community volunteers and anyone wanting to develop confidence or communication skills. There is no single Toastmasters type.

Do I have to be an extrovert?

No. Toastmasters is for introverts and extroverts alike. You can take part in a way that suits your personality and build confidence at your own pace.

What if English is not my first language?

You are welcome. Toastmasters gives people opportunities to practise speaking and listening in a supportive setting. Members have different communication styles and experiences, and learning from one another is part of the experience.

How often does Avon meet?

Avon Toastmasters meets on the first, second and fourth Tuesdays of each month at 7:00 pm at St Albans Community Centre, 1049 Colombo Street, St Albans, Christchurch. Please contact the club or check the official listing before travelling in case of a change.

How much does membership cost?

Membership fees and any joining costs can change. Please contact Avon directly for the current fee information before joining. As a not-for-profit club, fees support Toastmasters membership and club running costs.

What happens after I join?

A club officer will guide you through the membership process and help you get started with the Toastmasters learning programme. You can set goals, work through projects, take meeting roles and receive feedback. You are encouraged to learn at a pace that works for you.

What is Pathways?

Pathways is Toastmasters International's learning experience. It offers structured projects to help members develop communication and leadership skills. Members choose learning goals and practise them through speeches, evaluations and meeting participation.

Do I have to attend every meeting?

No. Members have different schedules and commitments. Regular attendance helps you get more opportunities to practise and contribute, but talk with the club about expectations and how to stay involved if you cannot attend every meeting.

Can I join if I only want to improve one specific skill?

Yes. You might want to prepare for a work presentation, become more comfortable speaking in groups, improve your listening, practise giving feedback or develop leadership skills. You can focus on goals that matter to you.

Where can I find more information?

Email avon@toastmasters.org.nz, call +64 21 913 105, message Avon on Facebook or Instagram, or view the official Toastmasters International club listing.
