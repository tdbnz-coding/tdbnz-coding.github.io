ABOUT OUR CLUB

A community where every voice can grow.

Avon Toastmasters is a Christchurch club where people come together to practise communication, build confidence and support each other's progress.

50+

PROUDLY PART OF CHRISTCHURCH

More than 50 years of helping people find their voice.

Avon Toastmasters was chartered on 1 September 1974 and has been part of the Christchurch community for more than five decades. Through changing times and generations, the heart of the club remains the same: people learning together, encouraging one another and becoming more confident communicators and leaders.

Our charter date is a milestone in a story shaped by the members who have contributed their time, ideas, speeches, encouragement and leadership over the years — and by the people who will join us next.

WHO WE ARE

Different people.
Shared ambition.

You might be preparing for presentations at work, finding your feet in a new role, studying, running a business or simply wanting to feel more comfortable speaking in a group.

Toastmasters offers a practical way to improve: you learn by taking part. Members support one another through prepared speeches, impromptu speaking, meeting roles and thoughtful evaluations. You can start small and build at a pace that suits you.

Avon is a local club in the worldwide Toastmasters network. Our meetings are organised by members and shaped by the people who take part.

OUR APPROACH

Learn by doing.
Grow together.

We believe the best way to become more confident is to practise in a place where it is safe to try, make mistakes, receive feedback and try again.
