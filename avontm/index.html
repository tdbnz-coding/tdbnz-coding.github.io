A PLACE TO GROW

Confidence is built.
Not wished for.

You do not have to be a confident speaker to join. You come to practise — and confidence grows with every opportunity.

At Avon Toastmasters, members take turns speaking, thinking on their feet, giving feedback and helping meetings run. You choose your own pace, try new skills in a friendly environment and learn alongside others who are developing too.

WHAT YOU CAN PRACTISE

Skills that travel
with you.

Whether you are speaking at work, leading a team, studying, volunteering or simply wanting to express yourself more clearly, the skills you practise here can make a difference.

01

Public speaking

Plan and deliver prepared speeches, tell stories and make your message clear.

02

Impromptu speaking

Think on your feet and respond to questions with greater ease.

03

Constructive feedback

Learn to notice strengths and offer practical, encouraging suggestions.

04

Leadership & teamwork

Take meeting roles, support others and practise leading in a real setting.

YOUR FIRST VISIT

Come as you are.
See how it works.

Being a guest is the easiest way to find out whether Avon Toastmasters feels right for you. You can watch, listen, meet people and ask questions.

1

Arrive and settle in

We will welcome you, introduce you to the club and help you feel comfortable.

2

Enjoy the meeting

3

Ask anything

Stay for a chat afterwards. There is no obligation to join or speak on your first visit.
