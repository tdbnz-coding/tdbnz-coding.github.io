Big ideas deserve
a confident voice.

You don't need to be a natural-born speaker. You just need a place to practise.

Avon Toastmasters is a welcoming Christchurch community where people from all walks of life develop practical communication and leadership skills. We learn by doing: trying speeches, thinking on our feet, taking meeting roles and supporting one another along the way.

Come as you are. Watch a meeting, meet our members and see how Toastmasters can help you make progress — at your own pace.

GOOD TO KNOW

First time?
We've got you.

A few answers to help you feel at home before you arrive.

Do I need experience in public speaking?

Not at all. People join Toastmasters for all sorts of reasons and with all levels of experience. The club is a place to practise, learn and build skills gradually.

Will I have to speak at my first meeting?

No. You're very welcome to observe, listen and meet people. You can participate if you want to, but there is absolutely no pressure.

What happens during a meeting?

Meetings generally include prepared speeches, impromptu Table Topics and encouraging evaluations. Members also take on small meeting roles to practise communication and leadership.

How much does it cost to visit?

Your first visit as a guest is free. If you decide to join, the club can explain current membership fees and the joining process.

When and where does Avon Toastmasters meet?

Avon Club is listed as meeting on the 1st, 2nd and 4th Tuesday of each month at 7:00pm at St Albans Community Centre, 1049 Colombo Street, Christchurch. Please check the official club listing before travelling.
