YOUR NEXT CHAPTER

Joining is simple. Start with a visit.

You do not need to be a confident speaker to join Toastmasters. You simply need to be curious and willing to give it a go. Come and see how our club works before making any decisions.

THREE EASY STEPS

From curious guest
to confident member.

There is no audition, no speech to prepare and no pressure to sign up on the spot. Take your time and decide whether Avon feels right for you.

01

Get in touch

Send us an email, call, or message us on Facebook or Instagram. Let us know you are interested in visiting and we can confirm the next meeting and answer any questions.

02

Visit as a guest

Come along to a meeting, meet members and see prepared speeches, Table Topics and evaluations in action. You are welcome to simply watch. Guest visits are free and there is no obligation to join.

03

Join when you are ready

If you decide Avon is the right club for you, speak with a club officer about the membership application, current fees and joining process. New members are introduced to the Toastmasters learning programme and supported as they get started.

WHO CAN JOIN?

New to speaking?
You belong here.

Avon welcomes adults from all walks of life, backgrounds and experience levels.

Some members want to feel more comfortable speaking in meetings. Others want to improve presentations, lead teams, tell better stories, think on their feet or simply challenge themselves. You do not need to be an extrovert or an experienced speaker. Toastmasters is designed to help you learn through practice.

Toastmasters clubs are open to people aged 18 and over. If you are unsure whether the club is right for you, visit and ask questions first.
