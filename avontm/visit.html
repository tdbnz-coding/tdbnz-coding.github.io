GUEST QUESTIONS

A few things you
might be wondering.

Do I have to speak at my first meeting?

No. You can simply observe. If you would like to participate, a member can explain what is involved.

Do I need public speaking experience?

Not at all. Many people join because they want to become more confident and practise in a supportive environment.

Is there any pressure to join?

No. Visit as a guest, ask questions and decide in your own time.

What should I bring?

Just yourself. You may wish to bring a notebook, but there is no special preparation required.

How do I confirm the next meeting?

Contact the club or check the official Toastmasters club listing before travelling.
