WHAT TO EXPECT

A friendly room. A clear structure. Plenty to discover.

If you have never been to Toastmasters, the format can seem unfamiliar. Here is a simple guide to what you may see during an Avon meeting.

01

WELCOME

Meet the people

The meeting begins with a welcome and introductions. A member can help you understand the programme and where to sit. As a guest, you can simply observe.

02

PREPARED SPEECHES

Ideas brought to life

Members deliver speeches they have planned and prepared. You will see different styles, topics and approaches — and how practice helps speakers improve.

03

TABLE TOPICS®

Think on your feet

Participants are invited to speak briefly on an unexpected question or topic. You are not required to take part as a guest; watching is absolutely fine.

04

EVALUATIONS

Feedback that helps

Speakers receive feedback that highlights what worked and offers ideas for their next step. Evaluation is a skill in itself: specific, thoughtful and supportive.

05

WRAP-UP

Reflect and connect

The meeting closes with a review and an opportunity to talk. Ask questions, meet members and find out how the learning programme works.

A NOTE FOR FIRST-TIME GUESTS

You don't need
to prepare a speech.

You can come along, take a seat and enjoy the meeting. There is no expectation that you will speak or join on the spot.

If you would like to participate in a small way, let a member know. We can explain the options and help you feel at ease.
